# Application Surface Brief

## Mode

Operate

## Task

Move from a portfolio of Japanese titles to a defensible India launch decision: submit a title, observe analysis progress, inspect score and evidence, compare scenarios, and review the launch playbook.

## Success

A first-time analyst can understand the system status, find or start an analysis, distinguish opportunity from confidence, trace conclusions to sources, and evaluate a launch scenario without training.

## Important States

Loading, empty portfolio, API unavailable, model unconfigured, source unavailable, queued, running, partially completed, failed, completed, scenario saved and validation error.

## Must Preserve

The FastAPI contract and backend-authoritative base result; Kizuna name; India-regional focus; evidence warnings; version metadata; user-selected Octolane visual reference.

## Direction Contract

WORLD: A calm editorial intelligence instrument on mineral paper, cut by one living spectral evidence line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The operating rail, connection state and portfolio decision field are visible together. The strongest current title appears as evidence under review rather than as a generic dashboard card.

VISITOR PATH: Portfolio → title intake → job progress → decision → regional evidence → scenarios → playbook.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The spectral signal line moves while an analysis job advances and settles when the result becomes reviewable.

CROSS-SURFACE REACH: The same rail, typography, ruled evidence rows and decision-sheet grammar extend across results, scenarios, methodology and future administration.

HONEST RISK: The visual calm can hide missing infrastructure unless model and source configuration states remain explicit and prominent.

QUALITY BAR: Octolane's spacing discipline, typographic authority and restrained motion, translated into an operational product rather than a marketing page.
